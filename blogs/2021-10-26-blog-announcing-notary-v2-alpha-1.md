Notary v1, otherwise known as Docker Content Trust, was released at at a time when there was one primary registry: Docker Hub. A lot has changed since then, some design choices around Trust on First Use (TOFU), key management, and a lack of content promotion within and across registries have become limiting factors for Docker Content Trust and Notary v1. At KubeCon EU 2019, container image signing was the main topic of interest for a small group in the community.
