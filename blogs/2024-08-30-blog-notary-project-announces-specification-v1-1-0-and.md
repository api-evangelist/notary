The Notary Project maintainers are excited to announce new releases, including Notary Project specifications v1.1.0 , notation v1.2.0 , notation-go v1.2.0 , and notation-core-go v1.1.0 . These versions are now ready for production use! Deprecation The experimental flag --allow-referrers-api used by notation sign and notation verify commands is now deprecated.
